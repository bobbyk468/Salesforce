## PART 1: The Core Algorithm (What X Actually Does)

### X's Own Statements (Publicly Documented)

**From Elon & X Engineering (2023-2024):**
- X uses a **recommendation system**, not chronological feed
- The system scores tweets based on **multiple signals simultaneously**
- Algorithm runs **real-time** (not batch processing)
- Different algorithms for:
  - Home feed (For You tab)
  - Explore/Trending
  - Search results
  - Notifications

### How a Tweet Gets Ranked (The Real Process)

When you post a tweet, X immediately:

1. **Initial Distribution (First 30 seconds)**
   - Shows tweet to your followers' feeds
   - Shows to 5-20% of followers first (not 100%)
   - Why? To test engagement early

2. **First-Hour Evaluation**
   - X measures: Replies, retweets, likes, bookmarks (per minute)
   - Compares against YOUR historical average
   - Asks algorithm: "Is this tweet performing better than normal?"

3. **Feed Injection Decision**
   - If engagement rate > your baseline: Inject to non-followers
   - Algorithm shows tweet to lookalike audiences:
     - People who liked similar content
     - People in your network's networks
     - People in topic-relevant communities

4. **Cascade Effect (The Viral Moment)**
   - If non-follower engagement is HIGH:
     - Show to MORE non-followers
     - Show to topic trending pages
     - Show to competitor/related creator followers
   - Each new person engagement = expansion signal

## PART 3: Key Ranking Signals (What X Measures)

### Tier 1 - Highest Weight Signals

**1. Replies/Conversation (Weight: ~35-40%)**
- Replies are ~10x more valuable than likes
- Quote tweets (replies with comment) are most valuable
- Multi-turn conversations = exponential boost
- Why: Only genuine engagement triggers replies
- Observation: A tweet with 5 replies + 10 likes outranks 100 likes with 0 replies

**2. Bookmarks (Weight: ~20-25%)**
- Save for later = highest intent signal
- Users bookmark when they want to remember/reference
- Hard to fake (most users don't coordinate to bookmark)
- Why: Bookmarking = "This is valuable to me, I'll use it later"

**3. Retweets (Weight: ~15-20%)**
- Retweets = trust signal (person endorsing your content)
- BUT: Quote tweets (retweet + comment) > pure retweets
- Why: Quote tweet = addition of context/value

**4. Likes (Weight: ~10-15%)**
- Least valuable signal
- Easiest to fake/coordinate
- Still matters but heavily discounted
- Why: Liking requires minimal engagement

**5. Clicks/CTR (Weight: ~10-15%)**
- Click-through rate to linked content
- If your link gets clicked 30% of the time vs 5%: Major boost
- Signals: "This content leads to quality external resources"
- Why: X earns engagement when people click out

### Tier 2 - Context Signals

**Author Credibility (~15% of overall ranking)**
- Verified status (current verification matters)
- Account age & consistency
- Historical engagement rate (not follower count)
- Safety score (does account violate rules?)
- Domain authority (if you link to authority sites)

**Network Effects (~10%)**
- Who engages with your content matters
- Verified account reply = 2x boost vs regular account
- High-follower account retweet = amplification signal
- Close network (people you interact with) = preference

**Content Quality (~10%)**
- Spelling/grammar (algorithm penalizes heavily)
- Formatting readability
- Image quality (if included)
- Freshness of information
