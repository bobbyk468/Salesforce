## COMMON X ADS SETUP QUESTIONS

### Q: How long until ads show?
**A:** 15-30 minutes after launch. First impressions usually come within 1 hour.

### Q: What if I get no clicks?
**A:** Most common reasons:
- Landing page isn't relevant to ad copy (fix: choose more specific page)
- Budget too low ($50 might only get 100-200 impressions)
- Targeting too narrow (fix: broaden keywords)
- Copy not compelling (fix: use different variant)

### Q: Can I pause/change ads mid-campaign?
**A:** Yes! Whenever you want. Common strategy:
- Day 1-3: Test all 5 campaigns
- Day 4-7: Double budget on top 2 performers
- Pause underperformers

### Q: How much should I spend first week?
**A:** Start with $250 test budget, then:
- If ROI positive (2+ clicks per dollar): Increase to $500/week
- If ROI breakeven: Keep at $250/week
- If ROI negative: Pause, redesign, relaunch

### Q: When should I increase budget?
**A:** After you see:
- CTR consistently >1% (good for paid traffic)
- Cost per click <$1 (profitable for awareness)
- Conversions happening (track form fills or email signups)
