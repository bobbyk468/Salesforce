A: Create media for 4-5 tweets today. Use the rest for tomorrow's posts. You have 21 posts across Week 1—plenty of time to batch media creation while posting.

**Q: What if my first post gets zero replies?**  
A: This is normal for a 12-follower account. Post anyway. By post #5-7, you'll see traction. Engagement is cumulative—your 5th post has better odds than your 1st.

**Q: Should I reply to comments if there are none?**  
A: No. But you WILL get comments. Questions always get replies. By your 5th post, you'll be replying to 3-5 comments per post.

**Q: Should I post all 3 times on Saturday/Sunday?**  
A: In Week 1, yes. Stick to the schedule. In Week 2 onwards, you can adjust based on data.

**Q: What if I miss a posting time?**  
A: Post as soon as you notice. X's algorithm is flexible with timing. 9:05 AM or 1:02 PM is fine. Consistency matters more than exact time.

**Q: Do I need to monitor every second?**
