## 📊 Why 3-4x Daily (Not 48x)

| Metric | 48x per day | 3-4x daily |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Follower perception | Spam | Expert routine |
| Algorithm favorability | Penalized | Boosted |
| Engagement per tweet | 0.6 | 3-5 |
| Follower quality | Low | High |
| Sustainability | Unsustainable | Long-term viable |

**Decision:** Post 3-4 times daily at optimal times when YOUR audience is active.

### Template Selection by Time

**9:00 AM ET — QUESTION POST (High Engagement)**

- Goal: Start conversation early

**5:00 PM ET — VALUE POST (Evening engagement)**
- Use: Template S1-S4 (Study tips) or C1-C3 (Community)
- Goal: Provide value before end of day
- Engagement expected: 5-10 replies
- Examples: "One thing changed my score", "Ask for help", "Celebrate wins"

**8:00 PM ET — OPTIONAL (Daily momentum)**
- Use: Quick wins (D1-D3) or rethread highlights
- Goal: Catch evening scrollers
- Engagement expected: 3-6 replies

## 🎯 Engagement Follow-Up Strategy

**Critical:** Reply to EVERY comment in the first 2 hours of posting

### Timing Strategy for Replies

**Within 30 minutes of posting:**
- Read all comments
- Reply to 3-5 top comments with value-add replies
- Goal: Signal algorithm that thread is active

**Within 2 hours:**
- Reply to all remaining comments
- Ask follow-up questions in your replies
- Like replies from others
- Goal: Maximize engagement thread count

**After 24 hours:**
- Stop replying to original post
- Move replies into new tweets/threads if needed
- Retweet good replies/comments

### Example Reply Strategy

**Original tweet:**
```
quick question: when you see 'supervisors manage queue', thinking:
A) Case Assignment Rules
B) Omni-Channel
👇
```

**Someone replies:**
```
@user: B, because supervisors have the freedom to work at their own pace
```

**Your reply (within 2 hours):**
```
@user exactly. and that's the KEY difference — it's about who controls it. assignment rules = admin controls everything. omni-channel = supervisors manage their own queue without admin help.

follow if you're grinding on ADM-201. more gotchas coming.
```

**Why this works:**
- Validates their answer (they feel smart)
- Adds more context (value-add)
- Invites follow (grows followers)
- Keeps thread active (algorithm boost)
