## BEST PRACTICES FOR INFLUENCER COLLABORATION

1. **Lead with Value:** Don't ask for favors. Offer first. "Free access for your community"
2. **Be Specific:** "I saw your thread on X and thought Y about Z" (not generic outreach)
3. **Make It Easy:** Provide discount codes, sign-up links, all promotional materials ready
4. **Amplify Their Work:** When they share our content, retweet, quote-tweet, thank them publicly
5. **Long-term Think:** Treat partnerships as ongoing relationships, not one-off transactions
6. **Track ROI:** Know which partners drive real value (use discount codes to track)
7. **Respect Boundaries:** If they say no or don't respond, move on (no persistence spam)
