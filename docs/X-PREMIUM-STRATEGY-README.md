## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: How long before I see ROI?**
A: Week 1 is testing. Week 2 you'll see patterns. Week 3-4 first partnerships. Month 2+ is where you see significant backlinks and traffic.

**Q: What if my ads aren't getting clicks?**
A: It's likely copy or targeting issue. See "Issue 1" and "Issue 2" in X-PREMIUM-STRATEGY.md Part 12 for fixes.

**Q: Should I do all 5 ad variants in Week 1?**
A: Yes, but only $50 budget each to test. Kill bottom 2 performers. Double down on top 2 in Week 2.

**Q: How many influencers should I target?**
A: Start with 20. Top 5-8 will respond. 2-3 will become active partners. That's normal funnel.

**Q: Can I do this solo or do I need a team?**
A: Solo possible (2-3 hours daily). Ideal: Campaign manager + content creator + outreach person.

**Q: What if I don't have graphics ready?**
A: Use Canva (free). Or hire designer for $50-100 upfront. Or screenshot your app + add text overlay.

**Q: How do I know if it's working?**
A: Track your numbers weekly. If doing better than X-PREMIUM-CAMPAIGN-TRACKER.md targets, it's working.
