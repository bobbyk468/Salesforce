## PART 8: MEASUREMENT & ANALYTICS

### X Premium Analytics Dashboard Setup

**Key Metrics to Track Daily:**

1. **Impressions** - How many people saw your tweet
   - Target: 500+ per promoted tweet
   - Organic posts: 1,000-3,000+ (depends on followers)

2. **Engagements** - Likes, retweets, replies combined
   - Target: 5-10% engagement rate
   - Organic threads: 20-50+ engagement per thread

3. **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**
   - Target: 2-5% for promoted tweets
   - Target: 1-3% for organic posts
   - Calculation: Clicks / Impressions × 100

4. **Cost Per Engagement (CPE)**
   - Target: $0.25-0.50 per engagement
   - Calculation: Budget spent / Total engagements

5. **Cost Per Click (CPC)**
   - Target: $0.50-2.00 per click
   - Calculation: Budget spent / Total clicks

6. **Follower Growth Rate**
   - Target: 50-100 new followers per week (organically gained)
   - Metric: New followers - Follower churn

## PART 12: RISK MITIGATION & TROUBLESHOOTING

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Issue 1: Low CTR on Promoted Tweets**
- Symptom: CTR < 2% after 100+ impressions
- Cause: Weak copy, wrong targeting, wrong landing page
- Solution: Kill the tweet, test new copy variation, ensure landing page matches offer

**Issue 2: High CPC but No Conversions**
- Symptom: Getting clicks but no sign-ups/resource downloads
- Cause: Landing page mismatch, confusing CTA, too many steps
- Solution: Simplify landing page, add clarity to CTA, reduce friction

**Issue 3: Poor Influencer Response Rate**
- Symptom: Reaching out to 10 influencers, only 1-2 respond
- Cause: Cold outreach, no pre-existing awareness, weak value prop
