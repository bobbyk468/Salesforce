Seventeen credentials. $12,900 if you sat all of them. And two of the seventeen you can't sit at all, which catches people out every year.

The Architect track is the most confusing part of the certification catalogue. Here's what it actually looks like, with real numbers.

## The two you can't book

[Application Architect](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/certifications/application-architect) and [System Architect](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/certifications/system-architect) have no exam. You earn them by collecting other credentials. That's why their prerequisite says "see component exams" and why their duration, question count and pass mark are blank in any honest dataset.

People try to book these. You can't. The $400 next to them is what the components added up to, not a fee you pay once.

Roughly: Application Architect comes from the app-side credentials, so Platform Data Architect, Platform Sharing and Visibility Architect, Platform App Builder and Platform Developer I. System Architect comes from the system-side ones, so Platform Integration Architect, Platform Identity and Access Management Architect, and Platform Development Lifecycle and Deployment Architect.

Check the current list on Salesforce's own page before you plan around it. The composition has changed before and will again.

## Why $12,900 is a misleading number

Three of the seventeen are the Certified Technical Architect stages.

| Stage | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| [Technical Architect (CTA)](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/certifications/technical-architect) | $1,500 |
| [Architect Evaluation](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/certifications/technical-architect-evaluation) | $1,500 |
| [Architect Review Board](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/certifications/technical-architect-review-board) | $4,500 |

That's $7,500. More than half the track, sitting in one credential that a few hundred people worldwide hold. The Review Board isn't multiple choice. It's a live session judged by sitting CTAs, which is why there's no question count or pass mark to publish for it.

Take the CTA out and the track is $5,400 for fourteen credentials. That's the number to budget against unless the board is genuinely your plan, and if it is, you want your employer paying.

## An order that works

Get Platform App Builder and Platform Developer I done first. They're listed as recommended rather than required, but in practice everything above assumes you have them.

Then pick a side. App-side if your work is data models, sharing and declarative design. System-side if it's integration, identity and release management. Don't alternate. The component credentials on each side feed each other, and you lose that if you zigzag.

Start with Platform Data Architect and Platform Sharing and Visibility Architect. Both pass at 58%, the lowest in the catalogue. Not because they're easy, but because finishing one early does something for your head that no study plan does.

Treat Integration Architect as the hard one. 67% to pass and a wide scope, and it's the one most people underestimate.

The CTA comes last, or not at all. It's a fine goal. It's a bad first one.
