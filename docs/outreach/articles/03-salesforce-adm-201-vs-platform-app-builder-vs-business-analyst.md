Same price. Same length. Same number of questions. On paper these three look interchangeable.

They aren't. The pass marks differ by five points, and the section weightings describe three different jobs.

Look at the pass marks. App Builder wants 73%, five points above ADM-201. On sixty questions that's three more right answers. Doesn't sound like much. It's the margin most people fail by.

## What each one is really testing

**ADM-201 is a breadth exam.** Eight sections, nothing dominant:

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Configuration and Setup | 20% |
| Object Manager and Lightning App Builder | 17% |
| Data and Analytics Management | 17% |
| Workflow and Process Automation | 14% |
| Sales and Marketing Applications | 11% |
| Service and Support Applications | 9% |
| Agentforce (new this release) | 8% |
| Productivity and Collaboration | 4% |

That Agentforce section is new in Summer '26 and it's worth five questions. Any study material written before this release doesn't mention it. Check the date on whatever you're revising from.

**App Builder is a depth exam pretending to be a breadth one:**

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Business Logic and Process Automation | 32% |
| Data Modeling and Management | 20% |
| Salesforce Fundamentals | 18% |
| User Interface | 17% |
| App Deployment | 13% |

Nearly a third of the paper is one section. Business Logic and Process Automation on its own outweighs ADM-201's bottom four sections put together. If flows, validation rules and knowing which automation tool to reach for aren't solid, breadth won't save you, and at 73% there's no room to carry a weak area.

**Business Analyst isn't a platform exam at all:**

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Collaboration with Stakeholders | 23% |
| Requirements | 18% |
| User Stories | 18% |
| Customer Discovery | 17% |
| Business Process Mapping | 12% |
| Development Support and User Acceptance | 12% |

There's no Configuration and Setup here. Over half the exam is discovery, stakeholders and requirements. Strong admins fail this one regularly, because they walk in expecting a victory lap and it's testing a different skill that happens to live on the same platform.

## So, which one

**ADM-201 first, if you're new.** Lowest pass mark of the three, it's the one job ads screen for, and the breadth gives you the vocabulary the other two assume you already have. It's also what Salesforce recommends before Business Analyst.

**App Builder next if you build things.** Objects, flows, Lightning pages. The overlap with ADM-201 is smaller than people assume. ADM-201 gives Object Manager and Lightning App Builder 17%. App Builder covers that ground across half the paper and goes deeper. Budget separate study time and respect the 73%.

**Business Analyst next if your job is closer to the business than the org.** Requirements, process mapping, running workshops. Different exam, different discipline, 72% to pass.

## The trap all three share

All three report a percentage per section and no overall score. So a fail tells you which sections were weak but not how close you came.

Because the weights are so uneven, 32% against 13% on App Builder, the section worth fixing is usually not your lowest score. It's the one where the points you can gain are worth the most.
