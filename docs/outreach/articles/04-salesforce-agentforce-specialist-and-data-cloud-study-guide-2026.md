Salesforce rebuilt its AI certification story in 2026: the AI Associate is
retired, Data Cloud is now Data 360, and Agentforce has gone from a product
name to a section weighting on the admin exam. If you came to this from the
ML/data engineering side rather than the Salesforce side, here is what the two
credentials that matter actually assess.

*By [Trailblaze Prep — Free Salesforce Certification Practice Questions](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/).*

## Agentforce Specialist

$200, 105 minutes, 60 scored questions, **72% to pass**. Platform
Administrator recommended first.

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| AI Agents | 35% |
| Prompt Engineering | 20% |
| Data 360 Fundamentals | 20% |
| Testing, Deployment, and Maintenance | 10% |
| Governance and Observability | 10% |
| Multi-Agent Orchestration | 5% |

Three things stand out if you have an AI engineering background.

**AI Agents at 35% is not about model selection.** It is about Salesforce's
agent abstractions — topics, actions, instructions, the boundaries between
what an agent may and may not do, and how it hands off. If you expect
questions about embeddings or context windows, you will be surprised. The
exam is about configuring a constrained agent inside a CRM, not about
building one.

**Prompt Engineering at 20% is templated, not freeform.** Prompt Builder,
grounding with record data, and the merge-field model. The skill being tested
is "will this prompt reliably ground on the right records" rather than
prompting technique in the general sense.

**Data 360 Fundamentals is a fifth of the exam**, which is the part most
people underprepare. You cannot pass Agentforce Specialist on agent knowledge
alone — a fifth of the paper is the data platform underneath it, and with a
72% pass mark you cannot afford to write off 20%.

Governance and Observability at 10% is small but reliably present: trust
layer, masking, audit. Multi-Agent Orchestration at 5% is three questions —
know the concepts, do not over-invest.

## Data 360 Consultant

$200, 105 minutes, 60 scored questions, **70% to pass**. Formerly the Data
Cloud Consultant — the rename is recent and most search traffic still uses the
old name.

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Data Activations and Utilization | 20% |
| Data Source Connection and Ingestion | 18% |
| Data Enhancements, Sharing, and Analysis | 18% |
| Harmonization and Unification | 17% |
| Solution Positioning | 14% |
| Data 360 Setup and Administration | 13% |

This reads like an ETL/CDP exam, and largely is — with one Salesforce-shaped
twist. **Harmonization and Unification at 17%** is identity resolution: match
rules, reconciliation rules, and the unified profile. If you have built entity
resolution before, the concepts transfer directly; the vocabulary does not,
and the exam tests the vocabulary.

**Solution Positioning at 14%** is the section engineers skip and then fail
on. It is scenario work: given a customer's situation, is Data 360 the right
answer, and which capability addresses the requirement. There is no technical
content in it at all.

## How the two fit together

Agentforce Specialist devotes 20% to Data 360 fundamentals; Data 360
Consultant devotes 100% to the platform that underpins it. Taking them in that
order — Agentforce first for breadth, Data 360 second for depth — works, and
the 20% overlap means the second one is cheaper in study time than it looks.

Going the other way works too and is arguably better if your background is
data engineering: Data 360 first plays to what you already know, and it turns
Agentforce's hardest-for-you section into its easiest.

## What changed in 2026, and what that means for study material

The [AI Associate](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/certifications/ai-associate)
is **retired**. It still appears in listings and in a great deal of blog
advice. Do not plan around it.

Data Cloud Consultant is now **Data 360 Consultant**. Same exam lineage, new
name; search engines treat the two names as different things, so a lot of the
best material is still filed under the old one.

Agentforce now appears as an **8% section on ADM-201 itself** as of Summer
'26. Admin study material written before that is missing a section worth five
questions.

More broadly: 34 Salesforce credentials have been renamed and 25 now have a
retired or closed exam. The canonical list is in the
[2026 Salesforce Certification Benchmark Report](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/salesforce-certification-report-2026),
with the data free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

## One thing to know about the result report

Salesforce gives you a percentage per section and no overall score. With
Agentforce Specialist weighted 35/20/20/10/10/5, averaging your sections will
mislead you badly — a strong showing on the three small sections is worth 25%
of the paper. The
[exam score calculator](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/tools/score-calculator)
does the weighted arithmetic for 50 exams including both of these.
