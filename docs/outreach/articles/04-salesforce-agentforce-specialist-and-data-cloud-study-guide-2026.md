Salesforce rebuilt its AI certification story this year. The AI Associate is retired. Data Cloud is now Data 360. Agentforce went from a product name to a weighted section on the admin exam.

If you're coming at this from data or ML rather than from Salesforce, here's what the two credentials that matter are really asking for. Short version: less about models than you'd think.

*From [Trailblaze Prep](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/), free practice questions and study guides for every Salesforce certification.*

## Agentforce Specialist

$200, 105 minutes, 60 questions, **72% to pass**. Platform Administrator recommended first.

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| AI Agents | 35% |
| Prompt Engineering | 20% |
| Data 360 Fundamentals | 20% |
| Testing, Deployment, and Maintenance | 10% |
| Governance and Observability | 10% |
| Multi-Agent Orchestration | 5% |

Three things will surprise you if you have an AI background.

**AI Agents is 35% and none of it is model selection.** It's Salesforce's agent abstractions. Topics, actions, instructions, what an agent is allowed to do and where it hands off. If you're expecting embeddings or context windows, they're not here. It's about configuring a constrained agent inside a CRM, not building one.

**Prompt Engineering is templated.** Prompt Builder, grounding on record data, merge fields. The skill being tested is whether your prompt reliably grounds on the right records. Not prompting technique as the wider world means it.

**Data 360 Fundamentals is a fifth of the paper.** This is the part people skip and then fail on. You can't pass this exam on agent knowledge alone, and at 72% you can't write off 20% of the questions and hope.

Governance and Observability at 10% is small but always there. Trust layer, masking, audit. Multi-Agent Orchestration at 5% is three questions. Learn the concepts, don't over-invest.

## Data 360 Consultant

$200, 105 minutes, 60 questions, **70% to pass**. This was the Data Cloud Consultant until recently, and most people still search for the old name.

| Section | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Data Activations and Utilization | 20% |
| Data Source Connection and Ingestion | 18% |
| Data Enhancements, Sharing, and Analysis | 18% |
| Harmonization and Unification | 17% |
| Solution Positioning | 14% |
| Data 360 Setup and Administration | 13% |

This reads like a CDP exam, and mostly it is. The twist is **Harmonization and Unification at 17%**, which is identity resolution. Match rules, reconciliation rules, the unified profile. If you've built entity resolution before, the ideas transfer straight across. The vocabulary doesn't, and the exam tests the vocabulary.

**Solution Positioning at 14%** is the one engineers skip. It's scenario work. Given a customer's situation, is Data 360 the right answer and which capability solves it. No technical content at all. Eight or nine questions you can lose by refusing to take it seriously.

## Doing both

Agentforce gives 20% of its paper to Data 360. Data 360 gives 100% of its paper to the platform underneath. So they overlap, and the second one is cheaper in study time than it looks.

Agentforce first then Data 360 works fine. But if your background is data engineering, go the other way. Data 360 plays to what you already know, and it turns Agentforce's hardest section into its easiest.

## What changed, and why your study material might be wrong

The [AI Associate](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/certifications/ai-associate) is retired. It's still in listings and still in a lot of advice. Don't plan around it.

Data Cloud Consultant is now Data 360 Consultant. Same exam lineage, new name, and search engines treat the two as different things, so a lot of the good material is still filed under the old one.

Agentforce now takes 8% of ADM-201 itself. Admin material written before Summer '26 is missing a section worth five questions.

Wider picture: 34 credentials renamed, 25 with a retired or closed exam. The list is in the [2026 Certification Benchmark Report](https://www.trailblazeprep.com/salesforce-certification-report-2026), free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

## One thing about the result report

Salesforce gives you a percentage per section and no overall score. With Agentforce weighted 35/20/20/10/10/5, averaging your sections will mislead you badly. The three small sections are a quarter of the paper between them, and doing well on those three while losing AI Agents looks fine on an average and isn't.
