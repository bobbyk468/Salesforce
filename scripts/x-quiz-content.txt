ADMIN QUIZ 4 — Sharing Rules vs Permission Sets
ID: quiz-admin-sharing-rules
================================================================================

[Tweet 1 — question]
#ADM201 exam question — drop your answer 👇

OWD = Private. a VP needs to report on records owned by people across different parts of the role hierarchy.

what do you configure?

A. Field-Level Security
B. Sharing Rules
C. Permission Sets
D. Restriction Rules
answer in 2 hours.

---

[Tweet 2 — answer]
answer: B. Sharing Rules

role hierarchy gives visibility upward. sharing rules extend access sideways or across branches.

common trap: people pick permission sets. permission sets control what you can do. sharing rules control what you can see. two different problems.

ADMIN QUIZ 5 — Flow Builder for Cross-Object Automation
ID: quiz-admin-flow-cross-object
================================================================================

[Tweet 1 — question]
#ADM201 exam question — drop your answer 👇

reps manually apply a discount to opportunity products after winning a deal. the manager wants it automated.

what's the right tool?

A. Approval Process
B. Formula Field
C. Flow Builder
D. Prebuilt Macro
answer in 2 hours.

---

[Tweet 2 — answer]
answer: C. Flow Builder

approval processes handle approvals. formula fields calculate but can't update related records. macros are for service reps clicking through tasks.

flow triggers on close, loops through products, applies the discount. declarative and clean.
